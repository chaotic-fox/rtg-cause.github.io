---

On 23 September 2026, a bachelor's thesis on debugging optimisation algorithms was successfully defended at the University of Bremen.

In this thesis,  a domain-specific language for the formal description and execution of optimisation algorithms in [EvoAl](https://evoal.de) was designed and implemented. The language lays the foundation for a protocol-based debugger that allows developers to step through an optimisation run and inspect its state, just like debugging a regular program. The work is a first step towards explaining the influence of the population and of randomness in population-based algorithms.

The thesis was supervised by [Rolf Drechsler](/people/drechsler) and [Bernhard J. Berger](/people/berger). Congratulations!
