At this year's Genetic and Evolutionary Computation Conference (GECCO 2026) in San José, Costa Rica, CAUSE was represented with a competition and two papers.

Supported by CAUSE, the competition [*Explainable Ride-Sharing Optimisation for Sustainable Traffic Organisation*](https://evoal.de/pages/competition26) invited participants to develop online optimisation algorithms for ride-hailing and ride-sharing in a plug-and-play simulation environment. In four tracks – static and dynamic environments for both ride-hailing and ride-sharing – the algorithms had to balance service quality, time efficiency, and operational costs. The competition was presented on 15 July, accompanied by a poster:

- Bernhard Berger, Christina Plump, and Rolf Drechsler: [*Explainable Ride-Sharing Optimisation for Sustainable Traffic Organisation*](https://doi.org/10.5281/ZENODO.21539325)

In addition, two CAUSE-related papers were presented on 13 July. [Christina Plump](/people/plump) presented the work of Lauren Paul, and [Bernhard Berger](/people/berger) presented his own paper:

- Lauren Paul, Christina Plump, Bernhard Berger, and Rolf Drechsler: [*Can Explainability Metrics Improve Genetic Programming? Lessons from 2048*](https://doi.org/10.1145/3795101.3814717)
- Bernhard J. Berger, Christina Plump, and Rolf Drechsler: [*Ride-Sharing Simulation for Optimisation*](https://doi.org/10.1145/3795101.3814733)
