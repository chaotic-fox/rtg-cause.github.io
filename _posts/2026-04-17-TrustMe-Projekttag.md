title: "TrustMe! Results at the Projekttag 2026"
---

On 17 April 2026, the 22 students of the bachelor project *TrustMe!* presented their results at the Projekttag of the University of Bremen.

Over the past two semesters, the students investigated how systems can explain their own decisions and suggestions. Their results were shown on four posters along with live demonstrations: three posters covered the application areas – explanations for game assistants, for negotiations between taxi agents, and for logistics – and a fourth poster presented the general methodology behind them.

Visitors could try out the demonstrations and discuss the explanations directly with the students. The day concluded with well-smelling waffles. Congratulations to all participants on their successful project!
