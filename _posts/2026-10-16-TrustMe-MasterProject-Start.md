title: "Master Project TrustMe! Has Started"
---

After the successful bachelor project, *TrustMe! – Self-Explainability of Systems in Complex Environments* continues: On 16 October 2026, five students started the master project at the University of Bremen, supervised by [Rolf Drechsler](/people/drechsler), [Caroline Dominik](/people/dominik) and [Christina Plump](/people/plump).

Over two semesters, the students will investigate which forms of explanation are helpful for users and for other systems, how a system can explain itself, and how algorithms must be designed for explainability. They can build on the results of the bachelor project – explanations for game assistants, for negotiations between taxi agents, and for logistics – or turn to new application areas. The project will probably also explore relations between explainability and security.

We are looking forward to two exciting semesters!
