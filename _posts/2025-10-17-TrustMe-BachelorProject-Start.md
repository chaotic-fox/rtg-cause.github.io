title: "Bachelor Project TrustMe! Has Started"
---

On 17 October 2025, the bachelor project *TrustMe! – Self-Explainability of Systems in Complex Environments* started at the University of Bremen. Over eight months, 22 students will explore how systems can explain their own behaviour and suggestions.

Electronic systems increasingly act as advisors – they suggest the next move in a game, plan routes, or help with programming. But when do we trust such suggestions? Explanations can create trust, provided they are understandable for their recipient, be it a human user or another system. Starting from simple game assistants, the students will investigate which forms of explanation are helpful, how a system can explain itself, and how algorithms must be designed to provide good explanations.

The project is supervised by [Caroline Dominik](/people/dominik), [Christina Plump](/people/plump) and [Rolf Drechsler](/people/drechsler). We are looking forward to the results!
