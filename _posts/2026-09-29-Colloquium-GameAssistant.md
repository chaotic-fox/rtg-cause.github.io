---

On 29 September 2026, a bachelor's thesis *"Application of Explanation Framework on Self-Explaining Game Policies"* was successfully defended at the University of Bremen.

In this thesis, a game assistant for the game 2048 with interpretable control policies was embedded into the formal framework for self-explanation by [Fey et al.](https://doi.org/10.1109/REW56159.2022.00023). In addition, a user study with about 25 participants to evaluate the usability of the explanations provided by the assistant was conducted. The study showed that explanations were usually requested when a suggestion was unclear – and that explanations could not fool users into accepting a bad policy, but provide assistance with good policies.

The thesis was supervised by [Rolf Drechsler](/people/drechsler) and [Bernhard J. Berger](/people/berger) and its results are in the process 
of being published at IEEE ICCE'26. Congratulations!
